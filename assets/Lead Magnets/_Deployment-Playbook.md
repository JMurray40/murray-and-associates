# Lead Magnet Deployment Playbook
### Murray & Associates, LLC — Bookkeeping and Advisory Services

**Purpose:** turn blog readers into email leads using the 11 tools you just built. This is the operating manual — what to gate, where to put the call-to-action, and the exact copy to paste into your form and delivery email.

**The one-line mechanic:** a reader finishes an article → sees an offer for the matching tool → gives you their email → the tool lands in their inbox → they're now on your list and you follow up. That's the whole funnel.

---

## 1. Gate it or give it away?

Not every tool should sit behind an email form. Two moves:

- **Gate (email required)** — high-intent tools someone only wants if they have the problem *right now*. These are worth an email address. Put them behind a form.
- **Ungate (free download, no form)** — quick, low-friction checklists. Giving these away builds goodwill, earns links/shares, and warms people up for the gated offers. Add a soft "want the full toolkit?" nudge instead.

**Recommended split:**

| Gate behind email | Give away free |
|---|---|
| 00 — Financial Health Checklist (master) | 03 — Monthly Bookkeeping Checklist |
| 01 — 13-Week Cash Flow Forecast | 04 — Monthly Close Checklist |
| 02 — Year-End Tax Prep Checklist | 06 — Bookkeeper vs. Controller vs. CPA |
| 07 — DIY Bookkeeping ROI Calculator | |
| 08 — P&L one-pager + Budget vs. Actual | |
| 09 — Chart of Accounts Starter | |
| 10 — Contractor 1099 & W-9 Tracker | |
| 05 — "Is It Time to Hire?" Self-Assessment | |

Rationale: templates and calculators signal buying intent (worth an email); short checklists are better as free trust-builders. The **master checklist (00) is your flagship gated offer** — promote it everywhere, not just one article.

## 5. Simple 3-email follow-up (after any gated download)

You've got the email — now earn the reply. Space these a few days apart.

1. **Day 0 — Deliver.** The template + one tip to use it (the template above).
2. **Day 3 — Be useful.** A short "most people get this one thing wrong" email tied to the tool's topic. No pitch. Link to the paired article.
3. **Day 7 — Soft offer.** "If your books feel like a second job, here's how I help." One clear line to book a call or reply. Point to the master checklist if they downloaded a single tool.

## 6. Where to host it (simple options)

- **Website + email tool:** put a form on a landing page; connect it to your email platform to auto-send the file. Works with most site builders (Squarespace, WordPress, Carrd) + an ESP (Mailchimp, ConvertKit, MailerLite).
- **HubSpot** (if you use it): native forms + a follow-up workflow that emails the file and starts the nurture. Cleanest all-in-one.
- **No-website minimum viable version:** a simple hosted form (e.g., a Google Form or a Bark.com profile link) that notifies you, and you send the file manually until volume justifies automation. Start here if you want to test demand this week.

**First move:** gate the master checklist (00) behind one form, link it from your homepage and email signature, and watch whether people opt in. That single test tells you if the funnel is worth building out.
